How we build

Three rules every app follows.

01

You are the baseline

Every number is compared with your own usual, not someone else's average.

02

Honest by design

No medical claims and no invented data. Missing data is shown as missing.

03

Made for every day

Quick to check, calm to look at, and useful on an ordinary Tuesday.
