Design concept · ongoing project
Telltale
it tells you before it happens — formerly "Project Instinct," name locked on sheet 07
A fitted suit that reads incoming motion, impact and proximity through a waist-mounted core hub, then pulses a haptic cue at the palm or sole that's about to matter — a fraction of a second before contact. Five glowing kite emblems mark the nodes: both palms, both soles, one larger hub at the waist.
What this actually is
An awareness aid, not a speed upgrade. The suit's own sense-to-cue loop (~30–50 ms) can't beat a spinal reflex — but it lands 150–350 ms before the wearer would otherwise consciously notice a hazard. That head start is the real, defensible claim. See sheet 05 for the numbers.
