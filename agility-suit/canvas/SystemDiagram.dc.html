The suit cannot beat a spinal reflex — it doesn't touch the muscles. What it can do is arrive 150–350 ms before the wearer would otherwise notice a hazard, turning a surprise into something half-expected. That's a head start, not superhuman speed — and it's the honest claim to build the pitch on.
