Naming directions
Telltale is locked — the rest stay here as the runners-up on record
Telltale
LOCKED — see sheet 01
Plain English, and it's the whole pitch in one word: a suit that tells you before it happens. Nobody else owns this as a hardware brand. Tagline writes itself — "it tells you before it happens." Not yet trademark-checked — do that before it goes anywhere public.
Kairo
From 回路 — Japanese for "circuit." Short, brandable, carries the anime-tech register the visual design already speaks. Sounds exotic without translating to anything embarrassing. Needs a real trademark check.
Vantage
A vantage is a position that gives you a wider view — which is the honest claim: more awareness, not more speed. Reads premium and plain-spoken rather than sci-fi; safest choice if the pitch leans toward investors over anime fans.
Warden
Implies something watching over you, always active in the background — matches the core hub's actual job description. Single hard word, easy to say out loud, hero-suit-appropriate without leaning on any specific IP.
Fivepoint
Literal and a little nerdy: five cue nodes, and the emblem itself is a four-point kite plus its own name as the fifth point of the joke. Best if the brand wants to lead with the engineering rather than the hero-suit fantasy.
NONE OF THESE HAVE BEEN CHECKED AGAINST EXISTING TRADEMARKS, GAME TITLES, OR PRODUCT NAMES — DO THAT BEFORE COMMITTING.
